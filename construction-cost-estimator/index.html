How to Use This Calculator

Follow these simple steps to get an estimate.

  1. Enter the wall length, height and thickness.
  2. Enter your brick dimensions and mortar joint thickness.
  3. Select the required cement-to-sand mortar ratio.
  4. Click Calculate Quantity to calculate estimated bricks, cement and sand.
  5. Enter your local material and labor rates.
  6. Add optional steel, aggregate and other project costs.
  7. Click Calculate Total Estimated Cost to view the approximate project cost.
🧮

Calculation Information

The estimator uses standard volume-based calculations.

Wall Volume Wall Length × Wall Height × Wall Thickness
Brick Quantity Wall Volume ÷ Effective Brick Volume
Mortar Calculated from the wall volume after accounting for brick volume and the selected cement : sand ratio.
Total Cost Material Costs + Labor + Other Additional Costs
