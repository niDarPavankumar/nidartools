User Guide

New here? These step-by-step guides will help you get the most out of every free tool on niDar Tools.

🖼️ Image Resizer & Compressor

Resize a photo to an exact size and file size — great for passport/ID photos.

  1. Open Image Resizer and upload your photo.
  2. Choose a unit (px/cm/mm/in) and enter your target width and height.
  3. Turn off "Maintain aspect ratio" if you need an exact custom size (like 3.5×4.5 cm for a passport photo).
  4. Set your target file size (KB/MB) and pick the output format (JPEG or PNG).
  5. Optional: turn on background options if required.
  6. Tap Resize & Compress, then Download File.

🔄 Image Format Converter

Convert images between JPG, PNG, and WebP formats directly in your browser.

  1. Open Image Format Converter and select an image.
  2. Choose the output format: JPG, PNG, or WebP.
  3. Tap Convert to process the image locally in your browser.
  4. Tap Download File to save the converted image.

Privacy: Processed locally on your device — never uploaded to a server.

📄 Image to PDF Converter

Combine one or more photos into a single PDF file securely.

  1. Open Image to PDF and upload your photos.
  2. Use the ▲ ▼ buttons to reorder the photos as desired.
  3. Choose page size (A4 / Letter / Fit to Image), orientation, and margin.
  4. Tap Generate PDF, then Download PDF.

🎂 Age Calculator

Find your exact age in years, months, and days instantly.

  1. Open Age Calculator.
  2. Enter your date of birth.
  3. (Optional) Adjust the "To Date" field if calculating for a specific date.
  4. Tap Calculate Age to view results and upcoming birthday details.

🔳 QR Code Generator

Create high-quality custom QR codes for any link or text.

  1. Open QR Generator.
  2. Type or paste your link or text content.
  3. Choose your preferred size and color settings.
  4. Tap Generate QR Code, then Download PNG.

🧩 Favicon & Icon Kit Generator

Turn a single logo image into a complete set of website icons.

  1. Open Icon Kit Generator and upload your logo.
  2. Enable background removal if your logo has a solid background.
  3. Tap Generate Icon Kit, then Download ZIP containing all standard sizes.

🪄 Background Remover

Remove backgrounds from photos instantly using on-device AI.

  1. Open Background Remover and upload your photo.
  2. Wait a few seconds for the browser-based AI model to process the image.
  3. Review the preview and tap Download PNG for a transparent background.

📃 Word to PDF Converter

Convert Microsoft Word (.docx) documents into professional PDF files.

  1. Open Word to PDF and upload your .docx file.
  2. Review the live preview rendered in your browser.
  3. Tap Download PDF to save your converted document.

📝 PDF to Word Converter

Extract text from text-based PDFs into editable Word (.docx) format.

  1. Open PDF to Word and upload your PDF file.
  2. Review the extracted text preview.
  3. Tap Download Word (.docx) to save the file.

📊 Percentage Calculator

Calculate increases, decreases, find value proportions and percentages easily.

  1. Open Percentage Calculator.
  2. Select your calculation type (e.g., X% of Y, or Increase/Decrease).
  3. Enter your values and tap Calculate to view instant results.

💰 EMI Calculator

Calculate loan EMIs, total interest payable, and repayment schedules.

  1. Open EMI Calculator.
  2. Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate (%), and loan tenure.
  3. Tap Calculate to check monthly breakdown and payment summaries.

🏷️ GST Calculator

Calculate Goods and Services Tax (GST) inclusive or exclusive amounts.

  1. Open GST Calculator.
  2. Enter the base amount and choose your applicable GST percentage rate.
  3. Select Add or Remove GST option and tap Calculate.

🏗️ Construction Cost Estimator

Estimate material quantities and approximate building costs.

  1. Open Construction Cost Estimator.
  2. Enter dimensions, area, and local material rate parameters.
  3. Tap Calculate Cost to view detailed expenditure breakdowns.

📝 Word & Character Counter

Track words, characters, sentences, paragraphs, and reading times live.

  1. Open Word Counter and type or paste your text.
  2. Use case-conversion tools or space cleaners if needed.
  3. View real-time statistics instantly on your screen.

🧾 JSON Formatter & Validator

Beautify, minify, and validate JSON data structures directly in-browser.

  1. Open JSON Formatter and paste raw JSON.
  2. Tap Format / Beautify or Minify as required.
  3. Use Copy Output to retrieve the clean JSON code.

🔐 Base64 Encoder & Decoder

Encode text/files to Base64 format or decode strings back to plain text.

  1. Open Base64 Encoder Decoder.
  2. Choose between Encode or Decode mode and input your data or file.
  3. Tap Convert and copy your processed result.

🔑 Password Generator

Create secure, random, and customizable passwords to protect your accounts.

  1. Open Password Generator.
  2. Set your desired length and character preferences (symbols, numbers, cases).
  3. Tap Generate Password and copy the secure output.

📑 PDF Merger & Splitter

Combine multiple PDF documents or split pages into separate files.

  1. Open PDF Merger Splitter.
  2. Select Merge or Split mode and upload your documents.
  3. Process files and download your finalized PDFs.

🤖 AI ATS Resume Checker

Check how well your resume matches target job descriptions for ATS compliance.

  1. Open ATS Checker.
  2. Paste your resume text and the target job description.
  3. Tap Analyze Resume to inspect scores, keywords, and improvement tips.

✨ Namocrux

Access special auxiliary utility features and developer tools.

  1. Open Namocrux page.
  2. Follow the tool guidelines to process your specific requirements.
