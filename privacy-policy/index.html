Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how niDar Tools collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Most tools on this website work directly in your browser. We generally do not require account registration to use our tools.

For image and file-based tools (such as Image Resizer, Image Compressor and Background Remover), your images and files are processed locally on your own device using your browser. These files are not uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to our servers. Once you close or refresh the page, any processed data is cleared from your device's memory.

AI-Powered Tools (such as the ATS Resume Checker)

Certain tools, such as the AI ATS Resume Checker, are not fully browser-based. For these tools, the text you submit (for example, your resume text and a job description) is sent temporarily to our server-side function, which forwards it to a third-party AI provider (Google Gemini API) to generate an analysis. The response is returned to your browser and displayed to you.

We do not store your resume text, job description or the AI analysis results in any database on our servers. This information is used only to generate your result and is not retained by us after the request is completed.

Because this data is processed by a third-party AI provider, it is also subject to that provider's own privacy policy and data handling practices. We recommend avoiding the submission of passwords, financial details, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive personal information into any tool on this website.

Cookies

We may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, remember preferences, and analyze website traffic.

Analytics

We may use analytics services to understand how visitors use our website so that we can improve our services.

Third-Party Services

Some pages may include third-party services such as analytics, advertising, or embedded content. These services may have their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Children's Privacy
