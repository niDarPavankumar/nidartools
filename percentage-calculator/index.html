Quick Reference

Percentage formulas

Simple formulas for common percentage calculations.

Percentage of a Number

X% × Y

Example: 20% of 500 = 100

What Percentage?

(X ÷ Y) × 100

Example: 25 is 25% of 100

Percentage Change

((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Positive means increase; negative means decrease.

Increase / Decrease

Original × (1 ± Percentage ÷ 100)

Add for increase and subtract for decrease.
