Understanding Construction Costs: Material, Labor & BOQ

Building a home or a commercial property requires strict financial planning. Without a proper Bill of Quantities (BOQ) and cost estimation, projects often go over budget. Our Pro Construction Cost Estimator helps you avoid this.

How is Construction Cost Calculated?

The total cost is generally divided into three major categories:

  • Material Cost (65%): This includes everything from cement, steel, sand, to finishing materials like tiles and paint.
  • Labor Cost (25%): The cost of masons, plumbers, electricians, and daily wage workers.
  • Architect & Miscellaneous (10%): Fees for design, approvals, and unexpected small expenses.

What is a BOQ (Bill of Quantities)?

A BOQ is a document used in the construction industry which itemizes materials, parts, and labor. Our online builder cost estimator automatically generates a basic BOQ for you based on standard industry practices.
