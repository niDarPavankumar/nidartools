GST Calculator: Free Guide

📄 Why Use a GST Calculator?

Calculating GST manually can be error-prone, especially when you need to quickly add GST to a quote or figure out the base price from a GST-inclusive amount. The GST Calculator does both instantly, for any of the standard Indian GST slabs.

🔄 How This Tool Works

Choose "Add GST" to calculate the final price from a base amount, or "Remove GST" to find the base price from a GST-inclusive amount. Enter the amount, select the GST rate, and get an instant breakdown of the original amount, GST amount, and total.

📌 GST Slabs in India

  • 5%: Essential goods and services.
  • 12%: Standard-rate goods.
  • 18%: Most goods and services fall under this slab.
  • 28%: Luxury and sin goods.

💡 Tips for Accurate GST Calculation

  1. Always confirm the correct GST slab for your product or service category.
  2. Use "Remove GST" when a price you received already includes GST and you need the base price for invoicing.
  3. Double-check calculations for high-value transactions.

🔒 Privacy & Performance

100% client-side calculation. Your amounts are never uploaded to a server or stored anywhere.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add GST to a price?

Multiply the base price by the GST rate and add it to the base price.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Divide the total price by (1 + GST rate/100) to get the base price.

What are the GST slabs in India?

The common slabs are 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%.
