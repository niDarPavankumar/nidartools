Mastering Compound Interest & FIRE Target

Compound interest accelerates your wealth growth because you earn returns not just on your initial investment, but also on the accumulated interest from previous periods.

What is the FIRE Movement?

FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early. The goal is to build a sufficient investment corpus so you can live off the returns without needing a traditional job. This is typically calculated using the 4% Rule.

How to Calculate Your FIRE Number

  • Step 1: Determine your expected monthly expenses in retirement.
  • Step 2: Multiply by 12 to find your annual expenses.
  • Step 3: Multiply the annual expenses by 25. This final number is your target FIRE corpus.
