Image Compressor: Free Guide

📄 Why Use an Image Compressor?

Large image files can slow down websites, exceed upload limits for government portals or job applications, and consume unnecessary storage space. An image compressor reduces file weight in KB or MB while keeping your photos looking sharp and clear.

🔄 How This Tool Works

Upload your image file, choose your compression level or target file size, and click compress to instantly process and download your optimized lighter image.

📌 Key Features & Tips

  • Quality Control: Balance compression ratios to fit portal upload requirements precisely.
  • Batch Optimization: Quick processing speeds for everyday use.
