📄 Why Use a Password Generator?

Weak or reused passwords are one of the most common causes of account breaches. A password generator creates truly random, unique passwords that are far harder to guess or crack than anything you'd come up with manually.

🔄 How This Tool Works

📌 What Makes a Password Strong

  • Length: Aim for at least 12-16 characters — longer is stronger.
  • Randomness: Avoid predictable patterns, names, or dictionary words.
  • Character variety: Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols.
  • Uniqueness: Use a different password for every account.

💡 Tips for Managing Passwords

  1. Use a password manager to store unique passwords for every account safely.
  2. Enable two-factor authentication wherever available, in addition to a strong password.
  3. Never reuse the same password across multiple sites.

🔒 Privacy & Security
