Understanding Mortgage Calculations (PITI)

Learn how to calculate your true monthly house payment.

What is PITI?

When you buy a house, your monthly payment consists of four main components, commonly known as PITI:

  • Principal: The portion of your payment that goes towards paying down the actual loan amount.
  • Interest: The cost charged by the lender for borrowing the money.
  • Taxes: Property taxes charged by your local government, usually divided into 12 monthly installments.
  • Insurance: Homeowners insurance to protect your property against damage.
