NamoCrux: Free AI Name Generator Guide

📄 Why Use an AI Name Generator?

Naming a business, app, brand, or project is often harder than it sounds — you want something unique, meaningful, and easy to remember. NamoCrux uses AI to turn a short description of your idea into a set of creative name suggestions, each with an explanation of why it fits, so you don't have to brainstorm alone.

🔄 How NamoCrux Works

Describe your idea — what it's for, who it's for, or the story behind it — and choose a naming style: Modern & Catchy, Traditional/Sanskrit-based, Short & Simple, or Deeply Meaningful. NamoCrux then generates a set of name suggestions, each with a short explanation of its meaning, so you can pick the one that fits best.

📌 What You Can Name

  • Business & Brand: Company names, brand identities, and creative brand ideas.
  • Startup & Product: Fresh startup concepts and product or service names.
  • App & Website: Memorable names for mobile apps, websites, and web projects.
  • Personal & Project: Names with personal meaning, for personal or professional projects.

💡 Tips for Better Suggestions

  1. The more detail you give about your idea, purpose, or story, the better the suggestions.
  2. Try more than one naming style — the same idea can lead to very different names.
  3. Use "Generate More" to explore additional options if the first set doesn't feel right.

🔒 Privacy & Convenience

NamoCrux is designed to make the naming process simple and clutter-free. Your recent searches are stored locally in your own browser for convenience, so you can revisit past ideas without creating an account.
