ATS Resume Checker: Free Guide

📄 Why Check Your Resume Against ATS?

Many companies use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to automatically scan and filter resumes before a human ever sees them. A resume that doesn't include the right keywords or has confusing formatting can get filtered out, even if you're a strong fit for the role. The ATS Resume Checker compares your resume with a job description to help you close that gap.

🔄 How This Tool Works

Paste your resume text and the job description you're targeting. The tool analyzes both, calculates an ATS match score, highlights keywords that are missing from your resume, and gives practical suggestions to improve alignment — all in your browser.

📌 What You Get

  • ATS Score: A score showing how well your resume matches the job description.
  • Keyword Matching: See which important keywords from the job description are present or missing in your resume.
  • Actionable Suggestions: Practical tips to improve your resume's chances of passing ATS filters.

💡 Tips for an ATS-Friendly Resume

  1. Use standard section headings like "Experience," "Education," and "Skills."
  2. Include keywords from the job description naturally, where they genuinely apply to your background.
  3. Avoid overly complex formatting, tables, or graphics that ATS software may not read correctly.
  4. Re-check your resume against each job description you apply to, since requirements vary.

🔒 Privacy & Responsible Use

Only provide information necessary for resume analysis. Avoid entering passwords, bank details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information.
