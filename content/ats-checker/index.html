ATS Resume Checker: Free Guide

📄 Why Use an ATS Resume Checker?

Most modern corporations use Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to automatically screen, parse, and rank resumes before a human recruiter ever sees them. An ATS checker helps you identify missing keywords and formatting flaws to pass automated filters.

🔄 How This Tool Works

Upload your resume document alongside the target job description text. The tool analyzes matching keywords, structural clarity, and formatting compatibility, providing actionable feedback to improve your score.

📌 Key Features & Tips

  • Keyword Matching: Highlights critical skills and terms missing from your resume compared to the job posting.
  • Format Analysis: Flags potential parsing issues like graphics, columns, or non-standard fonts.
  • Optimization Tip: Use standard text headers (e.g., "Work Experience", "Education") to ensure the ATS parser reads your data correctly.

🔒 Privacy & Performance

All resume processing and keyword comparison run entirely client-side on your device — ensuring your personal and career history remains strictly confidential.
