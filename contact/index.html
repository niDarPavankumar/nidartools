Helpful Information

Before You Contact Us

Including a few details can help us understand your request more quickly.

1

Name the tool

Tell us which niDar Tools page or tool you were using.

2

Describe the issue

Explain what you were trying to do and what happened.

3

Tell us your device

Mention whether you were using a phone, tablet or computer and, if possible, your browser.

4

Explain the result

Tell us what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
